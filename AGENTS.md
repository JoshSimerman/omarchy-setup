# Working agreement

- Discuss proposed machine changes with Josh before acting. Explain the scope
  and tradeoffs, then wait for explicit approval.
- Ask before installing any software, desktop, plugin, or package. Do not treat
  silence or an unanswered preference question as approval.
- Read-only investigation and preparation of a concrete proposal may proceed.
- Once an action is explicitly approved, complete that approved scope without
  repeatedly asking for the same permission.
- Record approved changes, verification, and undo steps in the relevant setup
  logs. Preserve other agents' uncommitted work.
